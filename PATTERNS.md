# Background Pattern Comparison

Three different background patterns to choose from, each with mathematical/probability connections:

## How to Switch Patterns

Edit line 7 in `index.html` to change the stylesheet:

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="styles-dots.css">
```

## Pattern Options

### 1. Regular Dot Grid (Currently Active)
**File:** `styles-dots.css` or `styles-enhanced.css`

**Pattern:** Uniform grid of dots, 24px spacing

**Visual:** Clean, professional, subtle texture

**Math connection:** Lattice structure

**Best for:** Most professional look, doesn't distract

---

### 2. Voronoi Tessellation
**File:** `styles-voronoi.css`

**Pattern:** Very faint Voronoi cell boundaries

**Visual:** Organic, sophisticated, unique

**Math connection:** **Your PhD dissertation topic!** Direct connection to your research on random Voronoi tessellations

**Best for:** Adding personal touch, conversation starter, shows your background

**Note:** Most thematically appropriate for your profile

---

### 3. Random Dot Spacing (Poisson Process)
**File:** `styles-random.css`

**Pattern:** Dots with random spacing (Poisson-like distribution)

**Visual:** More organic than grid, still very subtle

**Math connection:** Poisson point process, random spatial distributions

**Best for:** Balance between structure and randomness, probability theory connection

---

## Quick Switch Instructions

1. Open `index.html` in your editor
2. Find line 7: `<link rel="stylesheet" href="...">`
3. Change to one of:
   - `styles-dots.css` (regular grid - current)
   - `styles-voronoi.css` (Voronoi cells - your dissertation!)
   - `styles-random.css` (random dots - Poisson process)
4. Save and refresh browser

## My Recommendation

Try **`styles-voronoi.css`** - it's the most personally connected to your research and gives your site a unique, sophisticated look without being gimmicky. The Voronoi pattern is a subtle nod to your mathematical background.
