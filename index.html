About

I design and build AI solutions for governance analytics and policy research. Over the past six years, I've developed production systems including multi-agent workflows, RAG platforms, and knowledge graphs—working with diverse document databases including court judgments, legal argument transcripts, structured and unstructured data, reports, publications, and project lifecycle documents.

My background combines rigorous technical research with hands-on engineering. I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics (Probability Theory) from Indiana University and have since worked on responsible AI in high-stakes domains including bias detection in judicial systems. I specialize in translating complex research questions into scalable, production-ready systems.

I work with organizations including the World Bank Group, Global Environment Facility, and Paradigm Case Management (case management for district attorneys' offices in the US). My work typically spans the full solution lifecycle—from requirements gathering and architecture to deployment—with a focus on building systems that are both technically rigorous and practically useful.
